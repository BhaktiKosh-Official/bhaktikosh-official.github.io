Privacy Policy for BhaktiKosh

Effective Date: April 02, 2026
Developer: Sachin Dubey

1. Purpose of the App

Welcome to BhaktiKosh. The primary purpose of this application is to provide users with a clean, educational, and spiritual platform to read and listen to ancient Hindu literature, including Bhajans, Mantras, and Stutis. We are deeply committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a safe digital environment for all our users.

2. Data We DO NOT Collect

We respect your privacy. BhaktiKosh is designed to work seamlessly without requiring your personal details:

  • No Account Required: We do not ask you to sign up, log in, or create an account.
  • No Personal Data: We do not collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, physical address, or contacts.
  • No Voice Recording: While the app plays audio, it does not have access to your device's microphone and never records your voice or surroundings.

3. Data We Collect & How We Use It

To make the app function properly, we only utilize strictly necessary, non-identifiable technical data:

  • Local Device Storage: If you mark a Bhajan as a "Favorite" or change app settings (like font size or theme), this data is saved locally on your phone's storage. It is not sent to our servers.
  • Crash Analytics: We may automatically collect generic diagnostic data (like device model, operating system, and crash reports) solely to fix bugs and improve the app's performance.

4. Device Permissions Explained

BhaktiKosh asks for specific permissions only to enable core app features. Here is exactly why we need them:

  • Internet / Network State: Required to fetch updated Bhajans, daily quotes, and to load thumbnail images securely from our cloud hosting provider (Cloudinary).
  • Audio and Media Playback: The app uses Expo AV and Expo Speech (Text-to-Speech) libraries to play devotional audio tracks and pronounce Mantras. This permission is strictly for outputting audio, not recording it.

5. Third-Party Services

BhaktiKosh is built using modern frameworks. We use a few trusted third-party services that help run the app smoothly. These services may collect basic, anonymous usage data according to their own privacy policies:

  • Expo (Framework used to build and run the app)
  • Google Play Services (For Android system compatibility)
  • Cloudinary (Used strictly for hosting and loading app images/thumbnails)

6. Content Authenticity & Copyright

All content provided within BhaktiKosh is strictly verified. The literature (Bhajans/Stutis) consists of ancient works by revered saints (e.g., Tulsidas, Surdas, Mirabai) which belong to the Public Domain, or original creations by the developer. The app does not host or distribute copyrighted commercial music.

7. Children's Privacy

BhaktiKosh is a completely safe, family-friendly application. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has inadvertently provided any data, please contact us so we can resolve it immediately.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as we add new features to BhaktiKosh. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Effective Date." We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
