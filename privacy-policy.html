Privacy Policy for BhaktiKosh

Effective Date: 01 April 2026

1. Introduction & Purpose of the App

Welcome to BhaktiKosh ("we," "our," or "us"), developed by Sachin Dubey. BhaktiKosh is a dedicated devotional and educational mobile application designed to provide users with access to ancient Hindu spiritual literature, including Bhajans, Aartis, Mantras, and original devotional content. Our primary purpose is to promote spiritual well-being, cultural education, and peace.

We are deeply committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy strictly details what information is collected, how it is used, and the permissions required when you use the BhaktiKosh application (the "App").

2. Information We Collect (And What We Don't)

We believe in absolute transparency and data minimization. BhaktiKosh is designed to function with maximum respect for user privacy:

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We DO NOT require you to create an account, register, or provide any personal data such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address to use the core features of the App.
  • Local Device Storage: Any preferences you set within the App (such as saving "Favorite" Bhajans, app themes, or font sizes) are stored locally on your device's internal storage. We do not transmit or store this personal preference data on our external servers.
  • Automated Usage & Crash Data: To ensure the App remains bug-free and efficient, we may automatically collect anonymous diagnostic data. This includes device type, operating system version, crash reports, and generic app usage statistics. This data cannot be used to identify you personally.

3. App Permissions Explained

To provide a seamless and rich devotional experience, BhaktiKosh requires the following specific device permissions. We only request permissions that are absolutely necessary for the App's UI and features to function:

  • Internet & Network Access: Required to fetch dynamic content (like daily quotes, updated Bhajans) and to load thumbnail images securely hosted on our cloud servers (e.g., Cloudinary).
  • Audio & Media Playback: The App uses Expo AV and Expo Speech modules to play devotional audio, embedded videos, and provide Text-to-Speech (TTS) functionalities for Mantras.
    *Note: The App ONLY requests playback permissions. We DO NOT request microphone access, and we NEVER record or listen to your voice.
  • Wake Lock / Foreground Service (Optional): Required only if you are playing a continuous audio track or Bhajan so that the music does not stop when your phone screen turns off.

4. Content, Copyright & Intellectual Property

BhaktiKosh strictly adheres to intellectual property laws. The text-based content provided in the App falls into two categories:

  • Public Domain Content: Ancient texts, Stutis, and Bhajans written by historical saints (e.g., Tulsidas, Surdas, Kabir, Mirabai, Adi Shankaracharya). These are centuries old and legally reside in the public domain.
  • Original Content: Exclusive modern devotional texts marked as "(Original)" are authored specifically for BhaktiKosh and are the intellectual property of the developer.

We do not host or distribute copyrighted commercial audio tracks (such as Bollywood movie songs or private music label albums). If you believe any content within the App infringes upon your copyright, please contact us immediately for swift removal.

5. Third-Party Services & UI Links

The App is built utilizing the React Native and Expo frameworks. While the App's User Interface (UI) is custom-built, we rely on secure third-party infrastructure to deliver content safely. These services may collect generic device data as per their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services (For generic Android OS interactions)
  • Expo (Core app framework and crash analytics)
  • Cloudinary (For secure image/thumbnail hosting)

Additionally, if the App contains UI buttons linking to external platforms (such as YouTube videos or social media pages), please be aware that we do not govern the privacy policies of those external websites.

6. Security of Your Data

We value your trust. All network requests made by the App to fetch content are encrypted using standard HTTPS protocols. While we use commercially acceptable means to protect your experience, please remember that no method of electronic transmission is 100% secure.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

BhaktiKosh is a safe, family-friendly application containing purely spiritual and educational content. We do not display targeted advertisements, nor do we knowingly collect any personal data from children under the age of 13. The App is entirely safe for users of all age groups.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

As we add new features to BhaktiKosh, we may update our Privacy Policy. We will notify users of any significant changes by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this page. You are advised to review this policy periodically.
